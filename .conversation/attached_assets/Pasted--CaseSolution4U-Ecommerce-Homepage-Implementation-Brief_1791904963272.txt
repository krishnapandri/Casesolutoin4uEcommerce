# CaseSolution4U — Ecommerce Homepage Implementation Brief

Build a polished, responsive homepage for **CaseSolution4U**, an Indian ecommerce store selling mobile phone cases and accessories.

## Design inspiration
- https://www.thecasecompany.in/ — playful, aesthetic product discovery, colorful collections, and engaging merchandising.
- https://www.seasonmade.com/ — broad phone-accessory catalog, premium product presentation, device-specific navigation, and clear shopping information.

Use these sites as design references, not as templates to copy. Create an original brand identity.

## Brand and visual system
- Style: youthful, clean, modern, trustworthy, product-focused.
- Colors: warm white, blush pink, charcoal, and restrained lavender or sage accents.
- Components: rounded product cards, consistent spacing, subtle borders, soft shadows, clear buttons.
- Typography: strong readable headings and accessible body text.
- Avoid clutter, excessive emojis, intrusive popups, and unnecessary animation.

## Homepage sections
1. Promotional announcement bar, configurable by the admin.
2. Navigation with logo, categories, search, account, and cart.
3. Hero banner with the headline “Your phone. Your style.”, supporting copy, lifestyle/product image, and a Shop Now CTA.
4. Shop by phone brand/model.
5. Featured collections: Cute & Pastel, Minimal & Classic, Clear & Creative, Everyday Essentials.
6. Bestsellers or trending products loaded from the backend.
7. Accessory feature section.
8. Trust strip using only verified store policies.
9. Optional customer reviews, shown only when genuine reviews are available.
10. Newsletter signup, support links, FAQs, shipping/returns, policies, and footer.

## Product cards
Show product image, name, actual selling price, optional original price only when valid, stock status, and a link to the product page. Include device compatibility where relevant. Do not hardcode sample products or prices into the production catalog.

## Technical requirements
- Use the existing React frontend, Express backend, and PostgreSQL database.
- Fetch products and collections from the backend API.
- Keep the homepage responsive and accessible, with proper loading, empty, and error states.
- Use optimized WebP images where appropriate, descriptive alt text, and lazy loading for below-the-fold images.
- Use reusable components and maintainable styling.
- Connect search, product links, collection links, cart, and account controls to real functionality; do not leave decorative controls pretending to work.
- Never expose secrets or payment credentials in frontend code.
- Do not claim shipping, discounts, COD, returns, ratings, or security guarantees unless the store actually supports them.

## Admin and launch readiness
The existing admin panel should allow authorized staff to manage products, images, categories, inventory, homepage promotions, and orders. Protect admin routes and validate data on the server.

Deliver a cohesive homepage with realistic responsive layouts, production data integration, and a clear path to testing before launch.
